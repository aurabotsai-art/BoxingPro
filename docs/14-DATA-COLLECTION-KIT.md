# 14 — Data Collection Kit (the M2 afternoon, fully scripted)

_Everything needed to film the bootstrap dataset in one session. Target: ~2–3 hours of camera time per participant produces the deployment-matched data no YouTube video can (docs/07 §2.1)._

## Setup (10 min, once)

- Phone in **landscape**, propped/tripod at hip height, ~3 m back. Whole body + 0.5 m margin visible in frame at all times — check by shadowboxing the corners of your range.
- Highest fps the camera app offers at 720p or 1080p (60fps if available). Lock exposure/focus if the camera app allows.
- Light from the side or front, never a window behind you. Clear floor.
- Announce at the start of each clip (audio slate): participant code, stance, block ID from the matrix below. This makes labeling 5× faster.

## The matrix (per participant)

Film each block as its own clip (30–60 s each). Order matters — fresh technique first, fatigue blocks last.

| Block | Content | Reps/duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1–A6 | Singles: jab, cross, lead hook, rear hook, lead uppercut, rear uppercut | 15 slow + 15 fast each | Slow reps = phase-labeling gold |
| B1 | Feints + half-committed jabs | 20 | Announce "feint block" |
| C1–C4 | Combos: 1-2 · 1-1-2 · 1-2-3 · free combos | 10 each + 60 s free | |
| D1–D3 | Defense: slips L/R · rolls · duck + pullback | 15 each | Call them aloud before each rep |
| E1–E2 | Footwork: step-drag box pattern · pivots both directions | 60 s each | |
| F1 | Free shadowboxing, everything mixed | 3 × 60 s | The distribution anchor |
| **G1** | **Deliberate BAD reps**: drop hands after punches · overreach · square up · cross feet · lean back from imaginary shots | 10 each, announced | **The fault-exemplar set — do not skip; exaggerate freely** |
| H1 | Fatigue block: F1 repeated after 2 min max-output burpees/sprawls | 2 × 60 s | Powers fatigue_form_collapse thresholds |
| S1 | **Slow-mo ground truth**: native camera slow-mo (120/240fps), 10 fast jabs + 10 crosses, side view | one clip | THE calibration reference — non-negotiable |
| X1 | Second angle: repeat A1 fast jabs + F1 from 45° side | 2 clips | View-robustness data |

Per participant: ~35 min filming. Variety beats volume: 5 participants × 1 pass ≫ 1 participant × 5 passes. Recruit across heights/builds/skill if possible; absolute beginners are *valuable* (fault exemplars come free).
